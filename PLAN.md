### Core Administrative & Operational Features

* **Appointment Scheduling & Calendar Management:** Supports online patient bookings via web/app, automates SMS/email reminders (reducing cancellations by up to 30%), and integrates queue management to reduce waiting room times.
* **Electronic Medical Records (EMR) & Patient Profiles:** Centralizes patient histories (diagnoses, allergies, medications) into a unified digital repository with real-time updates and customizable templates for specific medical specialties.
* **Billing, Invoicing & Revenue Cycle Management:** Automates invoice creation (handling co-pays, insurance, and taxes like GST) and integrates with digital payment gateways, reducing billing errors by 20–25%.
* **E-Prescription Management:** Digitizes prescription sharing via email/app and links with in-house or external pharmacies to track inventory and automatically alert doctors to potential drug interactions.
* **Inventory & Pharmacy Control:** Monitors medication stock levels and expiration dates in real-time while generating automatic reorder alerts.
* **Insurance Claims Management:** Verifies insurance eligibility instantly and automates claim submissions to accelerate reimbursements.
* **Multi-Branch Support:** Enables multi-clinic operators to manage multiple locations, share patient records securely across branches, and view consolidated reporting from a single dashboard.

### Communication, Security & Business Intelligence

* **Patient Communication Portal:** Provides secure two-way messaging, health follow-up prompts, and educational content distribution.
* **Reporting & BI Dashboards:** Tracks patient volume, revenue analytics, and doctor performance through interactive data visualization tools.
* **Role-Based Access Control:** Limits data exposure based on staff roles (doctor, nurse, receptionist) using multi-factor authentication (MFA) and audit trails.
* **Security Compliance:** Built around strict regulatory benchmarks including HIPAA, GDPR, and ISO certifications using end-to-end data encryption.
* **Cloud Hosting & Backups:** Utilizes cloud architecture to eliminate local server maintenance costs while running daily automated backups.

### Emerging & Advanced Features

* **AI-Powered Predictive Analytics:** Uses past patient histories to predict appointment no-shows and proactively trigger automated reminders.
* **Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) Integration:** Synchronizes real-time health data (heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen levels) from wearable devices straight into the EMR.
* **Digital Consent Forms & E-Signatures:** Replaces paper form handling with automated, legally compliant digital signing workflows on tablets or mobile devices.
* **Integrated Diagnostics (Lab/Radiology):** Connects doctors directly with diagnostic labs using barcode tracking for samples and instant result updates to the patient profile.
* **Smart Marketing Tools:** Contains built-in utilities to segment patient databases and execute SMS or email campaigns for health tips, check-up promotions, and review requests.
